Based on scatter plot figure of Qi (qopen) vs Qc per frequency band., at low frequency central frequency 1.5 hz, the correlation is not good for low attenuation, but for higher attenuation, there is a linear relation of Qc and Qi, and Qi attenuation is generally higher. The correlation coefficient R is 0.77. While in higher cf, 63,6,12 hz, there is a clear linear relation between Qi and Qc, with R of 0.96, 0.97 and 0.96 respectivelly.
If we normalize the Qc and Qi value based on its median value for each frequency. The map in figure x showed that Qc and Qi has same spatial pattern for all frequency. high intrinsic attenuation zone like apennines and hellenides are shown clearly in both result.
Based on scatter plot of Qsc (qopen) vs peak delay, the relation between this parameter is not as strong as qi vs qc, with r of 0.53, 0.49,0.41, and 0.23 for each frequency. This is expected because this comparison use different approach to measure scattering, different with Qc vs Qi comparison, where both use similar approach (coda envelope fitting) but using different model (t-alpha for Qc and Paaschens RTE3D for Qi). While in Qsc vs peak delay, Qsc is obtained from RTE3D model, while peak delay is more simpler approach that measure the time delay for envelope peak. Both sensitive to scattering, but peak delay is more prone to noise and path or propagation effect. In 1.5 cf scatter plot, there is a linear relation between two parameters, which is shown by as Scattering attenuation is higher, the peak delay value tends to be higher too in most cases. Similar trend can be observed in 3 and 6 hz cf. However there is a high scattering anomalies from Campi Flegrei area that is observed in Qsc result, especially in this frequency band. These anomalies can be shown in the scatter plot as high Qsc, but the peak delay values is not that high. In higher frequency band cf12 hz, the correlation becomes lower, this may be caused the envelope in this frequency has more noise. For further comparison, side by side Qsc vs Peak delay surface map is also presented. Generally, the peak delay map is more spiky and contains more local anomaly. In 1.5 hz, the major high scattering features like apennines, albanides and hellenides can be distinctively shown in peak delay result.
