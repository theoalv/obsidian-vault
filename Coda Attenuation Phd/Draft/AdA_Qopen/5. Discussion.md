The qopen results is compared with Qc and peak delay result to see the confidence. The Qc analysis use late lapse coda envelope, while peak delay is fitted from the S wave and early coda.
Based on scatter plot figure of Qi (qopen) vs Qc per frequency band. at low frequency central frequency 1.5 hz, the correlation is not good for low attenuation, but for higher attenuation, there is a linear relation of Qc and Qi, and Qi attenuation is generally higher. The correlation coefficient R is 0.77. While in higher cf, 63,6,12 hz, there is a clear linear relation between Qi and Qc, with R of 0.96, 0.97 and 0.96 respectivelly.
