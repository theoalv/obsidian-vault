This g0 and b are parameters that can be directly translated  as scattering and intrinsic attenuation. At lower frequency of below 6 hz, we can observe a stable distinction between high and low attenuation zone (both g0 and b). in g0, apennines zone has the highest g0, followed by Adria Plate (po basin), hellenides, and albanides that have similar values. Alps, Dinarides and Pannonian Basin have the lowest g0 value in this region.
For the b parameter, apennines also has the highest b value, followed by albanides, even though the difference is not as wide as g0. then closely followed by hellenides, then adria plate. The Alps, Dinarides, and pannonian basin also have the lowest b values. However, pannonian basin has a slightly higher value. One thing to note that the evolution of values (pattern) by frequency of each region seems similar, which is increasing by frequency. However, Adria plate zone has a slightly different pattern. It is still generally increasing by frequency, but the changes is not as steep as the others (rather more flat).
At higher frequency of above 6 hz, the stability has changed for g0.
