
**Envelope shapes of same earthquake recorded in stations with different characteristics**

Fig. 6. from [[mayor_crustal_2016]] show coda envelope of same event recorded in 2 different stations (Po Plain and Central Alps).

![[Pasted image 20260720112851.png]]

We have a similar case from AdriaArray data, event 38630661 Mw 3, located in Southern Alps in boundary with Po Basin

![[Pasted image 20260720121557.png]]

Fitting in 6 hz central frequency band, in IV.MBAL (Central Alps) and IV.VMAN (Po Plain) both station have similar station-event distance (r). 
- At early coda envelope, IV.MBAL station has a strong S bulk energy, followed by quick decay (weak early coda energy) after S wave arrival.
- In contrast, IV.VMAN has weaker S bulk energy and strong early coda energy (slower decay)
- The late coda envelope in both stations have similar decay, which can be fitted with common slope.
![[Pasted image 20260720122327.png]]
Strong S bulk energy and strong early coda decay in the IV.MBAL (Alps) confirms that the region has low scattering (mean path length $l$), compared with IV.VMAN (Po Plain).
In a lower heterogeneity medium, most of the energy is not scattered, which resulted in higher bulk energy. Because there is minimum scattering, there is no scattered wave behind the direct S wave, which cause a strong coda decay after bulk.
Meanwhile, in medium with stronger heterogeneity, the probability of waves to be scattered is higher. As more energy become scattered, the bulk energy is decreased and transformed into coda energy. This is observable in IV.VMAN.


**Qc and Qi pattern difference in 1.5 hz band**
Based on scatter plot of Qi vs Qc in 1.5 hz band, a very low relationship between Qc and Qi is observed at lower attenuation range. From the comparison map, it is in the Alps region.
High attenuation zones that have similar pattern are apennines and hellenides region (with different level, Qi attenuation is higher)
