The Circum-Adriatic region is one of the tectonically complex areas in Europe [picardi2011], shaped by the convergence of Africa and Eurasia.  The region can be divided into several micro-plates around a shared continental foreland, the Adria Plate, which underlies the sorrounding orogenic belts [kissling2024]. Adria behaves as an independent microplate, moving separately from both Eurasia and Africa, with a lithospheric thickness of 70–120 km
