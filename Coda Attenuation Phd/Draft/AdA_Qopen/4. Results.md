Processed from 1.5 to 17 hz central frequency. All of the result are presented by log10 Q-1 normalized by the regional median values. The results displayed are at cf 1.5 hz, 3 hz, 6 hz and 12 hz.
intrinsic (Qi)
Shown a relatively similar pattern for all frequency. Apennines shown a high attenuation especially in the western area, where volcanic arc. The alps shown low intrinsic attenuation. Dinarides has low intrinsic, but become a little higher at higher freq. Dinarides has med-high attenuation, which become stronger as frequency increases. The high intrinsic zone in hellenides in low frequency concentrated along the subduction zone along the margin, while in higher frequency (above 6 hz cf) the central zone nearby agaean sea and nearby volcanic arc has higher attenuation which was previously low in lower central freq.
